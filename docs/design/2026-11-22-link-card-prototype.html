Link card

The card that opens on a document link or a smart chip: a hover card for the pointer, a preview dialog for click, Mod-K and Alt+Enter, and a sheet for a long-press on phones. All three share one body. The hover card and the dialog show the same three controls: Open, Copy Link and ⋯.
